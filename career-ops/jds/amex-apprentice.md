## Overview

US Consumer Services (USCS) drives profitable growth of the American Express US Consumer portfolio by acquiring customers and deepening Card Member engagement through differentiated products, partnerships, and membership services across Travel, Dining, Lifestyle, and Banking.

## Responsibilities

The Apprentice Program offers hands-on experience in analytics, risk management, and business problem solving alongside experienced professionals. Apprentices support ongoing projects, learn how data drives business decisions, and contribute to initiatives that enhance customer and business outcomes.

Teams include:

1. **Marketing Analytics** — revenue growth, customer acquisition, onboarding, engagement, retention, credit quality, and gaming minimization.
2. **Product & Membership Analytics** — profitable growth through product and membership development, loyalty, and engagement.
3. **Control Management** — risk mitigation and strengthening the control environment.

Typical responsibilities:

- Develop understanding of American Express business, products, and customer journey.
- Analyze business and customer data to identify trends, patterns, and insights.
- Support monitoring risks, identifying improvement opportunities, and solving business problems.
- Assist in preparing reports, presentations, dashboards, and business recommendations.
- Use AI-powered tools such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, and other LLMs for productivity, research, analysis, and content creation.
- Collaborate on analytics, process improvement, and business projects.
- Present findings and recommendations clearly and structurally.
