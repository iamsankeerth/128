## Role Type

**Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE)** — customer-facing technical role deploying in-house AI models into real production environments for creative partners and studios. Sits at the intersection of engineering, product, and delivery.

## About sync.

sync. is making video as fluid and editable as a word document — AI-modified video over AI-generated video. The founders are the original team behind **Wav2Lip**, the open-source lip-sync model with 9,000+ GitHub stars.

**Tagline:** AI lipsync tool for video content creators  
**Founded:** 2023  
**HQ:** San Francisco, CA (Hyderabad explicitly listed for this role)  
**Approach:** Curating high-quality datasets + training specialized models toward a generalized audio-visual model for understanding, modifying, and synthesizing humans in video.

**What they look for:** raw intelligence, boundless curiosity, exceptional resolve, high agency, outlier hustle.

## About the Role

Forward Deployed Engineers help deploy and shape sync.'s in-house AI models in real production environments. You will work closely with the internal team, creative partners, and major studios to adapt model behavior to project-specific needs.

This is a highly hands-on role for people strong at:
- Experimentation
- Workflow design
- Translating ambiguous creative or production goals into reliable outputs using trained models

You will sit at the intersection of **engineering, product, and delivery**:
- Work directly with models, refining performance for project-specific use cases
- Build repeatable systems
- Help partners get the most out of the technology in high-stakes production settings
- Move fast, think clearly, and thrive in collaborative project environments

**Notable client reference (public):** Operation Safed Sagar team.
