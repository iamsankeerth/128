## Job description summary

**Source:** LinkedIn posting (JR1084620) + aggregated Workday listing via YoHire; Workday page returned limited content on direct fetch.

Broadridge is hiring an entry-level **Member Technical** engineer at **Hyderabad Hi-Tec City** for freshers and recent graduates with **0–1 years of experience**. The role focuses on **front-end development with ReactJS** under senior engineer guidance, within Broadridge's global fintech platforms (investing, governance, communications).

### Required skills & qualifications

- 0–1 years of experience (freshers/recent graduates welcome)
- Good knowledge of **OOPS concepts** and basics of **ReactJS, NodeJS, JavaScript**
- Understanding of **software engineering fundamentals** and **data structures**
- Good written and verbal communication skills
- Strong learning mindset and collaborative team environment
- Basic understanding of **relational databases (RDBMS)** and **SQL queries**

### Key responsibilities

- Write clean, maintainable, and reusable code under senior engineer guidance
- Develop and enhance **front-end features using ReactJS**
- Collaborate with Product Owners and team members on business/functional requirements
- Participate in SDLC: coding, **unit testing**, debugging, deployment support
- Participate in **code reviews** and incorporate feedback
- Prepare and update technical documents, process notes, and deployment instructions
- Share regular progress updates with team leads and stakeholders

### Selection process (reported)

Technical interview covering JavaScript, ReactJS basics, OOPS, and data structures; managerial and HR rounds.
